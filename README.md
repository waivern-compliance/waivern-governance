# Waivern Governance Tool

A privacy and AI governance workflow platform: assessments, risk decisions,
approvals, recurring governance and a tamper-evident audit record. Multi-tenant,
with access scoped to legal entities within an organisation.

It integrates at the record level with the Waivern Compliance Portal, which
handles discovery and document generation.

## What it is, and what it deliberately is not

This is a **workflow and case-management** platform. It records what an
organisation processes, routes the decisions that have to be made about it, and
keeps an account of who decided what and when.

It is **not** a data discovery or content-scanning tool. It records *categories*
of personal data rather than indexing repositories, and it does not go looking
for unregistered AI usage. That boundary is deliberate: a platform that both
finds things and adjudicates them tends to do neither part well, and the
adjudication is the part that has to stand up afterwards.

Discovery belongs to the Waivern Compliance Portal, which feeds this platform
over a versioned API, and to scanning tools that push findings in. A formatted
DPIA is generated by the Portal from the facts recorded here. Two document
generators would eventually disagree, and then neither could be trusted.

## Features

- **Assessments** from one template engine: DPIA, UK TRA, EU TIA, AI risk
  assessment and screening questionnaires, with templates authored in the
  interface and no-account links for contributors.
- **Risk register** with ratings derived from likelihood and impact,
  mitigations, and attested risk acceptance with an expiry.
- **Workflow**: approvals routed by what was answered, tasks, service-level
  breach recording and recurring reviews, driven by an hourly sweep.
- **Registers**: Article 30 records of processing, third parties and their
  agreements, AI systems with their assurance chain, and a country library for
  transfer assessments.
- **Audit chain**: append-only and tamper-evident, with exports the recipient
  can verify for themselves.
- **Dashboards** and month-by-month trend reporting, derived from the records.
- **Integration API** in both directions: signed ingest from the Portal and
  scanners, a findings queue, incremental export and outbound webhooks.
- **Collaboration**: discussion with mentions on every record, personas that
  shape the home screen, a step-by-step guide for non-specialists and in-product
  help.
- **Optional AI assistant**, off by default and advisory only: it drafts,
  explains and reads agreements, and a named person accepts or rejects every
  suggestion.

[docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) explains why each part works the
way it does.

## Running it locally

Requires Node 24 (see `.nvmrc`), pnpm 10 and Docker.

```bash
pnpm bootstrap     # creates .env.local, generating AUTH_SECRET and INTEGRATION_KEY
pnpm db:up         # Postgres 16 on port 55432
pnpm db:migrate
pnpm seed
pnpm dev
```

Open http://localhost:3000. `pnpm seed` creates a demonstration organisation
with two legal entities and a handful of people at different levels of access,
and prints what it made. With `ALLOW_DEV_SIGN_IN=true` you can sign in as any of
them without a password, to see the same data as an administrator, as an
approver scoped to one entity, and as a contributor. The build throws if that
flag is ever set alongside `NODE_ENV=production`.

`AUTH_SECRET` signs sessions and `INTEGRATION_KEY` encrypts stored integration
credentials. Losing `INTEGRATION_KEY` makes existing stored credentials
unreadable, so in a deployment it belongs in a secret manager.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `pnpm test` | Integration tests. Needs the local database running. |
| `pnpm typecheck` | `tsc --noEmit`. |
| `pnpm lint` / `pnpm format` | ESLint and Prettier checks; `lint:fix` and `format:fix` fix what they can. |
| `pnpm audit:verify` | Recomputes every organisation's audit chain and reports breaks. |
| `pnpm db:generate` | Generates a migration from schema changes. |
| `pnpm db:reset` | Drops and rebuilds the schema. Destroys all data, audit chain included. |
| `pnpm seed:demo` | Loads a worked portfolio, so the dashboards have something to show. |
| `pnpm seed:countries` | Loads the shared country library. Reference data, not schema. |

## Deploying

The application is a Node process and a Postgres database, configured through
environment variables, with no platform SDK and no pinned region. It needs Node
24, PostgreSQL 13 or newer, an hourly scheduler for the sweep, TLS in front of
it, and at least one OIDC provider: there is no password login.

[docs/deploying.md](docs/deploying.md) covers migrations, scheduled work, health
checks and data residency, with worked examples for particular hosts.
